FAQ

My Pro purchase is gone.
On the upgrade screen that appears when you save a preset, tap "Restore purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID.

The click is quiet next to my music app.
Both share the phone volume. Use "Sound › Volume" in the app to balance the click.

Find BPM isn't accurate.
Tap mode is the most reliable — tap along at least four times.

What to include when you write
Your iPhone model, iOS version, what you tapped and where, and a screenshot if you can.
