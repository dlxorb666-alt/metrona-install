Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Metrona ("the app") has no accounts and does not collect or share your personal data.

1. Information we collect

None. The app does not collect your name, email, phone number, location or device identifiers, and it uses no ads, analytics or tracking.

2. Data kept on your device

  • Presets you create (tempo, meter, sound settings, name, color)
  • Song links or music files you add
  • Home layout and language

This stays on your device only and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. Microphone

Only in the microphone mode of "Find BPM", and only with your permission, the app listens for a few seconds to work out a song's tempo. The audio is analyzed on your device and is never recorded, stored or sent. You can turn microphone access off any time in iPhone Settings.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment details.

5. Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version and date on this page.
